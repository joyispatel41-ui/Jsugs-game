Before each fight you pick your monster: the Chainsaw Monster, or the Crush Monster with a giant hammer for a right hand. The camera locks onto the boss. Choose where you strike: ARM to blunt it, LEGS to slow it, HEAD or BODY to finish it.

You spawn in a safe hub. Press Go to the bosses, or walk into the red portal, when you are ready. Drag the left side of the screen to move and circle the boss. Dash through swings and jump the red shockwaves. Every kill levels you up and charges an ULT. Each boss gets a different ultimate, and every one tears off half its health.
